Short-form creators generally need three separate workflows: repurposing existing long-form content, editing native vertical video, and — for marketers — generating UGC-style ad creative without hiring actors. Different tools serve each one.

## Repurposing long-form video into Shorts

Opus Clip and Vidyo.ai both scan a long video (a podcast episode, a stream, a YouTube upload) and cut it into short, captioned clips automatically. They solve the same problem with different credit models — see the [Opus Clip vs Vidyo.ai comparison](/compare/opus-clip-vs-vidyo-ai/) for the specifics.

## Editing native vertical video

CapCut remains the default for creators editing directly on mobile — trending sounds, templates, and captions built for the format. Captions.ai takes a different angle: talking-head-style videos with automatic captioning and AI dubbing baked in, useful if you're recording pieces to camera rather than editing found footage.

## Generating video without filming

Kling AI's free tier is credit-renewing daily, which suits creators posting frequently and testing multiple concepts before picking one to refine.

## For marketers: UGC-style ads without actors

Arcads generates talking-head-style ad creative from a script, without booking a real actor — a distinct workflow from repurposing or editing, aimed at performance marketers who need ad variations at volume rather than organic posts. See [how to create UGC-style ads with AI](/guides/how-to-create-ugc-style-ads-with-ai/) for the full walkthrough.

## Putting it together

A typical creator stack looks like: film or generate the raw clip, edit it in CapCut or Captions, and — if the source is a longer piece — run it through Opus Clip or Vidyo.ai first to find the best moments. Check each tool's profile for current free-plan limits before building a workflow around it.
