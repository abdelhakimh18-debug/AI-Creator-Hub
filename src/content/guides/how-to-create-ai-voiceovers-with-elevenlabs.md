# How to Create AI Voiceovers with ElevenLabs

A strong AI voiceover workflow begins with a script written for speech rather than for silent reading.

## Workflow

1. Edit the script for natural spoken rhythm.
2. Add punctuation and paragraph breaks where narration should pause.
3. Choose a voice appropriate for the subject and audience.
4. Generate short sections first so pacing and pronunciation can be reviewed.
5. Fix names, numbers, technical terms, and unnatural emphasis before generating the final narration.
6. Normalize the completed voiceover with the rest of the video's audio mix.

## Narration checklist

Use short paragraphs, clear punctuation, consistent pronunciation, and deliberate pauses. Always listen to the full exported track before publishing or delivering it to a client.
