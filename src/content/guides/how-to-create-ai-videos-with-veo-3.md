A reliable Veo 3 workflow starts with a clear shot plan and short, precise prompts. The goal is to control subject, action, camera, environment, lighting, and audio without overloading a single prompt.

## Step 1: Plan the shot

Write down the subject, location, action, camera movement, lighting, mood, and duration. Keep each generation focused on one visual beat.

## Step 2: Establish continuity

For recurring characters, keep identity references, clothing, age, environment, and visual style consistent. Reuse the same descriptive language across related shots.

## Step 3: Write the prompt

A useful structure is: subject + action + environment + camera + lighting + style + audio requirements. Avoid adding unrelated details that do not affect the shot.

## Step 4: Generate and review

Check faces, hands, objects, wardrobe, camera movement, and unwanted modern elements. Regenerate only the problematic shot instead of rebuilding the whole sequence.

## Step 5: Edit the sequence

Combine the strongest clips, then add narration, music, sound effects, captions, and transitions in the editing stage.

Product capabilities and access rules can change. Verify current Google terms, availability, and commercial-use conditions before using the workflow for paid client work.
