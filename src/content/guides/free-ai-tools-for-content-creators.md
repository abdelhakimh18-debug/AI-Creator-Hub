Most AI tools for creators offer a genuine free tier, not just a trial — the difference matters, because a trial expires and a free tier doesn't. This is a starting list organized by what you're trying to make, not a ranking.

## Video generation

Kling AI's free tier renews daily credits rather than granting a one-time allowance, which makes it the most sustainable free option for ongoing experimentation. Pika and Runway both offer monthly credit grants instead — smaller in total volume but still enough to test a workflow before paying.

## Video editing

CapCut is free-to-start with no core paid gate for basic mobile/desktop editing. If you need browser-based editing with AI captions, most competitors gate their AI features behind a paid tier faster than CapCut does.

## Voice and audio

ElevenLabs' free plan includes a monthly credit allowance you can actually use in a real project, not just a demo. Suno and Udio both offer daily-renewing free credits for music generation, though commercial use of the output requires a paid plan on both.

## Image and design

Adobe Firefly and Leonardo AI both have usable free tiers for AI image generation. Canva's free plan covers most day-to-day design needs (thumbnails, social graphics) without ever requiring a card.

## Repurposing and clips

Opus Clip's free plan includes a monthly minute allowance for turning long videos into short clips — useful for testing the workflow before committing to a paid plan.
